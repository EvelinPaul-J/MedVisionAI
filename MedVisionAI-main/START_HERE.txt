RETINAREACH AI — WINDOWS QUICK START

1. Open this folder.
2. Double-click run_app.bat.
3. Wait for: Uvicorn running on http://127.0.0.1:8000
4. Open Chrome and go to: http://127.0.0.1:8000
5. Start Screening -> register patient -> Fundus Image Capture.
6. Click Open Camera for a webcam/UVC fundus camera, OR choose a dataset image.
7. Click Run Quality Check + AI Analysis.
8. The app moves automatically through Quality Check -> AI Analysis -> Result.
9. Generate PDF Report from the result screen.

If a fundus camera is connected, it must be exposed to Windows/browser as a
webcam/UVC video device. The app shows a camera selector when more than one
camera is available.

No separate React/Node build is required.
